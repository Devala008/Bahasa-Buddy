Why learn with us

Made for travelers, not textbooks

This isn't an academic grammar course — it's practical conversation you can use the same day, taught patiently for absolute beginners.

Absolute beginners welcome

Zero Indonesian needed. We start with the most basic words and sentences and build up slowly, at your pace.

Phrases you'll use today

Every example comes from real traveler situations: ordering food, haggling at the market, chatting with your driver.

Small groups, real talking time

Classes stay small so you get plenty of speaking practice, not just listening. Gentle correction as you go.

Built around your trip

Morning, afternoon, or evening — sessions fit your travel plans. One-off lessons or a short series, your call.

Take-home phrase notes

After every session you get a simple recap of phrases to review before your next chat with a local.

Friendly, patient teachers

No judgment on mispronunciation — our teachers love helping travelers try, laugh, and improve together.

What you'll learn

Phrases you'll actually use around Bali

Eight core topics — take them in order from the start, or pick the one you need most before your next outing.

01

Greetings & small talk

Say hello, introduce yourself, and keep a short chat going.

02

Getting around

Ask for directions, distances, and how to get somewhere by car or bike.

03

Markets & bargaining

Ask prices, haggle politely, and know when a deal is fair.

04

Ordering food & drinks

Read a menu, ask about spice level, allergies, and get recommendations.

05

Hotels & homestays

Check in, ask about breakfast, wifi, and request what you need.

06

Numbers & money

Count, understand prices, and handle everyday transactions with ease.

07

Common signs & situations

Understand everyday signs, warnings, and simple written Indonesian.

08

Polite requests & thank-yous

Ask nicely, say thanks, and leave a warm impression with locals.
